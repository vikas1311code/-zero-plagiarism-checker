Plagiarism is the practice of taking someone else's work or ideas and passing them off as one's own. It is considered a serious ethical violation in academic and professional contexts.

Academic integrity refers to the ethical policy or moral code of academia. It includes values such as avoidance of cheating or plagiarism, maintenance of academic standards, honesty and rigor in research.

Copyright infringement occurs when a copyrighted work is reproduced, distributed, performed, publicly displayed, or made into a derivative work without the permission of the copyright owner.

Intellectual property refers to creations of the mind, such as inventions, literary and artistic works, designs, symbols, names and images used in commerce. It is protected by law through patents, copyright and trademarks.
